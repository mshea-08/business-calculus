# Linear Models and Differential Calculus

These notes were created to accompany a course on linear models and an introduction to differential calculus at PLU. The content in these notes were largely drawn from the textbooks:

- <a href="https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Calculus/Applied_Calculus_(Calaway_Hoffman_and_Lippman)">Applied Calculus</a> by Calaway, Hoffman, and Lippman,
- <a href="https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Applied_Mathematics/Applied_Finite_Mathematics_(Sekhon_and_Bloom)">Applied Finite Mathematics</a> by Sekhon and Bloom. 

Learners should be comfortable with topics from college algebra. Below is a breakdown of the information covered in these notes:

1. **Linear Functions.** A review of lines, including applications, and solving linear systems in two variables. 
2. **Matrices.** An introduction to matrix algebra, solving linear systems using matrices, and an introduction to Markov Processes. 
3. **Polynomials and Rationals.** An overview of polynomial and rational functions. This includes solving for zeros, graphing polynomials and rationals, asymptotic behavior of functions, and applications. 
4. **Exponents and Logarithms.** An overview of exponents and logarithms. This includes properties of exponents and logarithms, applications of exponential models, and graphing exponentials and logarithms.
