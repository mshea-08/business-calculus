# Linear Models and Differential Calculus

These notes were created to accompany a course on linear models and an introduction to differential calculus at PLU. The content in these notes were largely drawn from the textbooks:

- <a href="https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Calculus/Applied_Calculus_(Calaway_Hoffman_and_Lippman)">Applied Calculus</a> by Calaway, Hoffman, and Lippman,
- <a href="https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Applied_Mathematics/Applied_Finite_Mathematics_(Sekhon_and_Bloom)">Applied Finite Mathematics</a> by Sekhon and Bloom. 

Readers should be comfortable with topics from college algebra. Below is a breakdown of the chapters included:

1. **Linear Functions.** Lines, linear systems, and their applications. 
2. **Matrices.** Matrix operations, solving linear systems using matrices, matrix inverse, and an introduction to Markov Processes. 
3. **Polynomials and Rationals.** Determining zeros, graphing functions, asymptotic behavior, and applications.
4. **Exponents and Logarithms.** Properties of exponents and logarithms, exponential models, and graphing functions. 
5. **Derivative Basics.** Limits, continuity, interpretation of the derivative, the limit definition of the derivative, and basic applications of the derivative. 
6. **Derivative Rules.** Basic derivative rules, product rule, quotient rule, and chain rule.
7. **Optimization.** Interpretability of the second derivative, finding extrema, and applications of optimization.
